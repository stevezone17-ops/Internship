{# ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── SECTION 5: ENTERPRISE SECURITY ARCHITECTURE (#security) ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
VERIFIED ARCHITECTURE

Enterprise security at every level.

Simple Banking implements genuine, production-grade security patterns across cryptography, rate limiting, and state verification.

Cryptographic PIN Security

Transaction PINs and user passwords are never stored in plain text. They are hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and salt.

Brute-Force Rate Limiting

Automated defensive tracking locks accounts after 5 consecutive failed attempts, preventing automated dictionary and credential stuffing attacks.

Immutable Audit Trail

Every transaction, deposit, withdrawal, login, and profile modification is recorded in an immutable ledger with timestamps and origin data.

Atomic Balance Integrity

Double-entry transactions are validated server-side. Transfers are executed atomically to ensure funds cannot disappear or create negative balances.

Simulation Disclosure

Educational Banking Platform Notice

Simple Banking is a simulated fintech environment. All accounts, account numbers, balances, and transactions depicted on this platform are demonstration records intended solely for software engineering education, architectural inspection, and UI/UX design testing.

This application does not hold banking licenses, does not process real currency or government-issued tender, and is not affiliated with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC), or any other monetary regulatory authority. Never enter real banking credentials, PINs, or confidential financial information.

Terms of Simulation

By accessing the Simple Banking simulation platform, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • All funds and transactions are fictional and cannot be redeemed for fiat or digital currency.
  • You may not use this system for unlawful activities, automated spam, or abusive denial-of-service testing.
  • Data stored within demo databases may be reset or purged periodically during automated testing cycles.

Privacy & Data Handling

We respect user privacy within our simulation environment:

  • Password credentials and transaction PINs are stored exclusively as one-way salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes.
  • We do not sell, transfer, or distribute user registration records to third-party commercial entities.
  • Simulated transaction logs are maintained strictly for educational demonstration of double-entry ledger systems.

Security Standards

Our application implements multi-layered security controls:

  • PBKDF2-SHA256: Industrial-standard cryptographic key derivation for all credentials.
  • Brute-Force Protection: Account lockout initiated after 5 consecutive failed authentication attempts.
  • CSRF Mitigation: Cryptographic token verification on every state-mutating HTTP POST request.
  • Atomic Operations: Non-negative balance constraints enforced at the database driver layer.
